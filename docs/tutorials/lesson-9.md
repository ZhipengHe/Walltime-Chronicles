## 📋 What You'll Accomplish (planned)

By the end of this 15–20 minute lesson, you'll have:

- [ ] **Run the agent in the right place** — on your own machine, not on the login node
- [ ] **Connected it to Aqua** — SSH access, and what it cannot reach
- [ ] **Written its rules** — an `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md` for an HPC repo
- [ ] **Drawn the line** — what to delegate, and what stays yours (`qsub`, `qdel`, the resource spec, the job count)
- [ ] **Checked before the queue** — pre-flight a job on the desk before it waits hours to fail
